# One matchup. Settle it.

## Ships
- Share any revealed matchup as a one-round grudge match.
- Beat-my-score links preserve the exact five matchups and A/B sides, with no scores in the link.
- Shared challenges have isolated saved progress and never affect daily streaks.
- Funnel events distinguish first picks, finishes, copied links, and completed native share sheets. A copied link is not a confirmed post.

## Launch Post
Your league chat has opinions. Give it a matchup.

Sunday Split now lets you share one real fantasy matchup, lock in a pick, and settle the argument with the reveal. Or send your exact five and ask: can you beat my score?
