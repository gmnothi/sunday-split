# Your week, wrapped

## Ships
- Rolling seven-day accuracy, days played, hints used, biggest miss, and confidence outcomes.
- Position alignment is defined as how often your chosen lineup won that position group, not an invented position prediction.
- Downloadable spoiler-free PNG and shareable recap.
- Records are device-local, deduplicated on reload, and begin after this feature ships. No fabricated backfill.
- Next-day-return event uses Toronto calendar days, not elapsed 24-hour sessions.
